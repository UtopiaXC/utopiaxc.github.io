Hi there, I am from Dalian, China, you can also call me UtopiaXC. I previously worked as a Senior Engineer at a listed automotive provider, where I led a team in the development of vehicle systems. Currently, I am pursuing a Master of Engineering in Information Systems at the University of Tsukuba, Japan. My research focuses on Agent-Based Modeling (ABM) and Evolutionary Computation (EC). I am currently collaborating with Johns Hopkins University on an infectious disease simulation model for the World Health Organization. Additionally, I am researching the application of ABM and EC in economy systems to simulate assets market fluctuations caused by macroeconomic regulation policies, with the goal of reverse-iterating to find out more effective economic policies.
